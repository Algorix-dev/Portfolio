Personal Development Operating System

A gamified self-improvement platform that treats your life like a strategy game. Instead of a boring to-do list, GrowthOS gives you an operating system for becoming who you want to be — with XP, streaks, skill trees, and a full analytics dashboard.

Daily habit tracking with streak counters and completion rates
Goal hierarchy: Daily tasks → Weekly → Monthly → Long-term vision
Daily reflection journal to turn experience into learning
Analytics dashboard showing habit trends and skill growth over time
Identity system — align your daily actions to who you're becoming
